Ski Instructor · Winter 2022/23, 2023/24

As a CSIA-certified ski instructor at WinSport, I taught students ranging from 3-year-old beginners to adults, enforcing safety protocols on the hill and adapting each lesson to dynamic snow and weather conditions in real time.

The role trained me to break a physically complex skill into small, teachable steps and explain it differently depending on who was in front of me, a direct parallel to communicating technical ideas clearly to different audiences. It also built comfort with staying calm and making quick judgment calls under changing, sometimes unsafe conditions.
