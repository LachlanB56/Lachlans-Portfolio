Pedalheads

Calgary, AB

Bike Instructor · Summer 2023, 2024

At Pedalheads I instructed children ages 5–12 in cycling fundamentals and roadway safety, running group lessons where every student was progressing at a different pace and needed a different level of support.

This meant constantly reprioritizing attention across a group while keeping safety the top concern. It was good practice in staying adaptable and level-headed when managing several moving parts at once, which carries over directly to hands-on lab and hardware work where conditions change and small mistakes have real consequences.
